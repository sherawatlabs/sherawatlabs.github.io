Privacy Policy

Cobra Tangle • Sherawat Labs • Effective November 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sherawat Labs handles information when you play Cobra Tangle (Effective Date: November 15, 2026).

1. Local-Only Game Storage

Cobra Tangle does not require account registration, Google Sign-In, or cloud login. Your Career level progress, high scores, tokens, unlocked skins, Prestige unlocks, achievements, Daily Challenge streaks, and sound/theme settings are stored strictly locally on your device via browser/WebView local storage. Sherawat Labs does not collect or upload your local save data to external game servers. Uninstalling the app or clearing device storage will permanently erase local progress.

2. Google AdMob & Consent Management

Cobra Tangle uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to display rewarded and interstitial advertisements. Depending on your region and consent choices, Google AdMob may collect or process advertising identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP-derived coarse location, app interaction events, and crash/diagnostic signals to serve personalized or non-personalized ads and prevent ad fraud.

3. Your Privacy Choices

Where regional consent applies (such as the EEA and UK), you may review or update your ad consent preferences at any time from the Settings menu ("Privacy & Ad Consent Choices") or manage ad personalization in your Android device settings.

4. Children's Privacy

Cobra Tangle does not knowingly collect personal information such as names, email addresses, or contacts from players.

5. Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests, contact Sherawat Labs at sherawatlabs@gmail.com.
